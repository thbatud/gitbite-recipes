# Mexican Bean and Potato Bake

![](mexican-bean-and-potato-bake.jpg)

## Ingredients

- 3 fairly large potatoes, cut into bitesize pieces
- 2 tablespoon oil
- 1 onion
- 1 bell pepper (I used red), cut into bitesize pieces
- Small can of corn
- 1 medium courgette (zucchini), cut into bitesize pieces
- 2 cloves of garlic
- 1 hot chili
- 400 g tin kidney beans, drained (240g, or ~ 1 ¼ cups, when drained)
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 400 g tin chopped tomatoes (~ 1 ⅓ cups)
- 2 teaspoon smoked paprika
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1 teaspoon oregano
- Salt
- Black pepper
- 75 g cheddar cheese, grated (~ ¾ cup when grated)
- Fresh coriander (cilantro), to serve (optional)

## Instructions

1. Toss the diced potatoes in a dash of oil, and spread them out in a large casserole dish or baking dish. Roast at 190°C (Gas Mark 5 / 375°F) for around 45 minutes, tossing halfway, or until mostly cooked and starting to crisp up.
2. Meanwhile fry all of the vegetables. Thema add the kidney beans, tinned tomatoes, and spices. Season generously, and mix to combine. Summer for 10-15 minutes. Add to potatoes.
3. Top with the grated cheese, and bake for 15 more minutes, until the cheese has melted.
